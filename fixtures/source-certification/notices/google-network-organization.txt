Google-owned IP address ranges

Google publishes this list of IPv4 and IPv6 address ranges. VanSail IP Atlas
reports Google as the network organization for matching address blocks.
The observed fixture contains four original records and their edition header.

The VPC guide defines the ranges and their scope. Its documentation license
applies to the guide. The address file has no separate data license. VanSail
records educational use under Google's published terms and credits Google in
the global dataset notices.

https://docs.cloud.google.com/vpc/docs/access-apis-external-ip#ip-addr-defaults
