NRO Extended Statistics Report

Source statistics are published by the Number Resource Organization and
AFRINIC, APNIC, ARIN, LACNIC and RIPE NCC. VanSail IP Atlas extracts the
registration country of the organization to which each eligible IP allocation
or assignment was made. Geographic use of those addresses may differ.

The NRO publishes this report for research into Internet number resources.
VanSail records the educational-use decision for the five registries'
contributions. The global report does not specify a uniform open-data license.

APNIC makes its delegated statistics available for download and use on the
condition that it accepts no liability for loss or damage arising from their
use. APNIC gives no guarantee of accuracy.
