The RIPE NCC publishes the daily IPv6 split files. VanSail IP Atlas extracts
registered network identifiers on public IPv6 allocations and assignments.
These registrations may have changed after the observed edition. The adjacent
fixture retains three institutional examples with the original file notice.
It excludes contact references, free-text descriptions and remarks.

Article 3 of the RIPE Database Terms and Conditions includes scientific
research into network operations and topology. Article 4.5 limits compilation,
redistribution and reuse to an insubstantial part unless RIPE NCC permission
is granted. The RIPE NCC retains rights under Article 9. The project owner
has recorded an educational-use decision for the compiled facts. No separate
permission for substantial reuse has been verified.
