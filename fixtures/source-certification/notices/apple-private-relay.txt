Apple Inc. publishes the iCloud Private Relay egress IP ranges so that
network operators can recognize connections from the service. VanSail IP
Atlas uses the listed address prefixes as positive evidence of proxy egress.

Apple retains its rights in the original material. Its website terms limit
republication and automated access. The project records its educational-use
decision and credits Apple. The review found no separate dataset license.
