Mullvad VPN AB publishes the city locations of its relay servers. VanSail IP
Atlas uses these observations only for the listed entry addresses. Map points
represent the city center. The project records educational use of these facts
under its source policy. Mullvad's published service terms describe use of
its VPN. A separate grant for the relay dataset has not been established.
